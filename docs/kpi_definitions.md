# KPI definitions

- **ARR:** Sum of contracted recurring revenue normalized to one year.
- **CLV:** Expected gross profit attributed to the average customer relationship.
- **Churn rate:** Customers lost during a period divided by customers at period start.
- **Gross retention:** Starting recurring revenue retained, excluding expansion.
- **Net revenue retention:** Starting recurring revenue retained, including expansion and contraction.
- **Health score:** A 0–100 behavioral score combining usage, recency, orders, and support friction.
